# The courtyard game

## Pillars

- A small walled place that has stood a long time and is kept up by hand.
- Everything is made of wood, rope, canvas and iron, and shows its age.
- Night is warm, close and quiet.

## Mood

The end of a long day at a frontier gate: tired, watchful and friendly. A person standing here should feel looked after by people who have little and share it.

## Palette

Weathered brown, faded canvas, iron grey and lamp amber. Saturated primaries and anything that glows by itself never belong here.

## Light

Light comes from oil lanterns and fire: low, amber and unsteady. It reaches a few metres and the rest is left in shadow.

## Shapes and materials

Chunky, hand-made shapes with worn edges. Nothing is glossy, nothing is plastic and nothing is made by a machine.

## Sound

Wind, a creaking sign, a distant hammer. Nothing electric hums here.

## Do

- Do light every lit place with warm light: a colour temperature between 1800 K and 2700 K.
- Do give every flame-lit lantern a soft flicker: its flicker setting is on.
- Do keep the reach of every light between 2 m and 5 m.
- Do have the gatekeeper's greeting mention the toll, using the word "toll".
- Do put one oil lantern (`lantern_oil`) at every market stall: as many lanterns as stalls.
- Do give every market stall its own canvas awning (`awning_canvas`): as many awnings as stalls.

## Don't

- Don't place a `neon_sign` anywhere.
- Don't place a `plastic_chair` anywhere.
- Don't place a `traffic_cone` anywhere.
- Don't place a `vending_machine` anywhere.
- Don't use an exclamation mark in any spoken line.
- Don't tint a light: its tint stays `white`, and its warmth comes from its colour temperature alone.

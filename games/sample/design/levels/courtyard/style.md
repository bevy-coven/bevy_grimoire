# The courtyard

## Pillars

- A walled yard with one gate, where people stop on their way through.
- The market in the south-west corner is the busiest corner, and it is tidy.
- The gate is the one place that is always watched.

## Mood

Settled and watchful. By day it is a working market; at night it is a few lamps, the gate and its keeper.

## Palette

Faded canvas over weathered wood. The gate is darker iron. The only warm colour is lamp amber.

## Light

At night the gate is lit by two lanterns, one on each side, hung low. Beyond their reach the yard is dark. The market keeps its own lanterns, one to a stall.

## Shapes and materials

The market's stalls stand shoulder to shoulder in one row with their canvas overhead. Crates and barrels stand against the stalls, never in the lane.

## Sound

Low voices at the gate, canvas moving in the wind.

## Do

- Do light the gate with exactly two lanterns, one on each side of it.
- Do keep the gate lanterns' brightness between 400 lm and 800 lm.
- Do stand a market's stalls in one row along its south edge, between z = 8.5 and z = 9.5.
- Do turn every market stall to yaw 180° so its front faces the lane.
- Do have the gatekeeper address a visitor as "friend".
- Do have the gatekeeper's greeting say the gate shuts at "dusk".

## Don't

- Don't let a lantern flicker faster than 6 Hz.
- Don't raise the night's ambient light above 5 lux.
- Don't hang a lantern higher than 2.5 m.
- Don't have the gatekeeper say "welcome".
- Don't have the gatekeeper mention the crown.
- Don't place a `pennant_bright` in the market.

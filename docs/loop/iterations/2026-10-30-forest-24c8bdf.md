# 2026-10-30 forest: the planted forest is drawn with bevy_flora's own trees (#102 slice 6)

Focus: family F6 (#102, own claim) · Commits: 06f6e5b, 24c8bdf (on main: c5b09a8e..6e512827) · Machine: bmb (Mac, w6) · Outcome: shipped

## Summary
`forest_view.rs` (behind `--features bevy_flora`) adds `bevy_flora::FloraPlugin` to the editor and draws the open map's `forest/<map>.ron`: a scattered tree is a `PooledTree` of its species' `TreePool` built in the background (the issue's decision: the crate's pools and LOD), and a placed tree is a `ProceduralTree` grown from its species with its own seed and `TreeAge`. It polls the forest file and every `.tree.ron` its species name twice a second, like `terrain_view`, so a `tree_edit` regrows the trees that use the file. That meets #102's last test acceptance, "the tree regrows"; the clip is owed (`not yet recorded`), and by the owner's rule a missing clip does not hold the issue open.

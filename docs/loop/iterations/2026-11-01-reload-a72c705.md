## Summary
`standards.ron` was loaded through a Bevy `AssetLoader` with Bevy's file watcher, beside `FileWatch` and the editor's stat poll, and the two were measured on Linux (big) and macOS (bmb): every write is seen by every watcher, in place and by rename, so H2's concern did not materialise; but a save of several files reaches the asset system spread over more than one frame while `FileWatch` says one reload is due after every save, a test needs the wall clock, and on macOS Bevy's watcher thread panics for a root that is not canonical. The guide says keep the watchers and when to read it again; the spike stays behind the `asset_spike` cargo feature with its tests. #204 closes with `Fixes #204`: both acceptance items are met in a72c705.

## Target: E-024 (migrate one keys::Action into the operation registry)
Candidate: pin/unpin a placement (`Action::OwnToggle`, the O key), verb `placement_set_pin`, undo through the registry as `Undo::Pin`, mirroring `map_set_settings` / `Undo::Settings`.

What was proven before blocking:
- New agent test `an_agent_can_pin_a_placement` (3 tests): failed before (`placement_set_pin` is not a known operation), passed after the registry op and editor wiring were added.
- The editor's pin, unpin and reason paths dispatched the operation through `set_pin`.

What blocked it:
- `operations_have_two_front_ends::the_allowlist_of_unmigrated_actions_only_shrinks` asserts `UNMIGRATED.len() == Action::ALL.len()` and that every variant is listed by name. `Action` is generated by `enumerated!` (keys.rs ~504–833). Removing `OwnToggle` from `UNMIGRATED` therefore fails the ratchet unless the variant leaves the enum.
- Removing the variant means binding O outside `keys::Action`. That bypasses the `keys::Context` Typing guard, so O would pin while a name is typed. The alternative is redesigning keymap bindings to name operations. Both are design decisions beyond one verb. Logged as E-025.
- The ratchet was not weakened. `UNMIGRATED_MAX` and the test were not changed.

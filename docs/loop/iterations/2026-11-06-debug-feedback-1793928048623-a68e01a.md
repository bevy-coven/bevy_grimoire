# feedback-1793928048623 — the Pieces panel draws past itself

**Classification:** `bug`. *"This whole panel has text running off of it, and looks squished and jumbled"*
says how the panel **is**.

**Outcome:** `blocked`. Seen in the capture, not testable in the harness, and the live reproduction could not
reach the capture's screen at all. No fix, nothing shipped, no changelog line.

## The oracle that would have been used

**`containment`** (and, for the jumbled rows, the companion non-overlap): every text rect lies inside the panel
that clips it, and no two rows of one list share a vertical band. Read from `ComputedNode` +
`UiGlobalTransform` so a restyle cannot satisfy it.

## Why it is blocked rather than fixed

`capture_feedback_1793928048623` was written and **passes on the base commit** — in the headless rebuild the
panel contains its text and its rows do not overlap. Per §6 that is `seen, not testable`, so the test was
**not committed**: a test that will not fail is not a proof.

The live route failed for a reason worth recording, and it is probably shared with
`feedback-1793927950267`: **on the installed 0.2.9 the Meshes tab cannot be reached.** Clicking it does
nothing, and the `door_open` operation reports `outcome: "previewed"` with `would_change: {door: kit}` and
never applies — the door stays `MAP`. Whatever stops the click reaching that tab is the same class as the
"What's new" tab that does not respond, and **that** is the thing to fix first: it blocks reproduction of
this capture and probably several others in the queue.

## What this repo did

- Reproduced nothing, and says so rather than shipping a guess.
- Recorded the attempt and the observed `door_open` behaviour, which is itself a finding.
- Left the ledger row `blocked` with that pointer.
- Shipped no test (it does not fail), no fix and no changelog line.

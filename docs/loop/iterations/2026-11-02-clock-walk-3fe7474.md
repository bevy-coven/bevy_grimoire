## Bugs fixed
**#271 (67c1084) — the clock edit steps the exposure.**
Must-fix: blocks the clock and season demos, common (every clock/season demo), no workaround (a clip would
show a white screen).
Root cause: `apply` wrote `*clock = settings.clock.clone()`, a step; the crate adapts `Exposure.ev100`
with `EXPOSURE_TAU_S = 1.5`, so a noon→dusk step is a dusk scene at noon's exposure for 1.5 s.
Fix: `ClockRamp` + `walk_the_clock`, started only when the settings differ **only** in the hour
(`differs_only_in_hour`); `apply` then leaves the hour to the walk and writes every other field whole.
The walk takes the shorter arc (noon→20:00 is +8, noon→06:00 is −6) and lands exactly, so no second walk.
Regression check: `crates/bevy_cauldron/tests/a_clock_edit_walks_the_hour.rs` (4, `--features bevy_elements`).
**Failing-first proved**: with the walk turned into a jump, `the clock moved 8.00 hours in one frame;
[12.0, 20.0]`. Live on big: **0.0 % white in all eight samples** while the picture falls 90.4 → 76.0;
before, 12 frames at 45–54 %.

## Enhancement: a spanned settle in the demo checker (dc2a022)
A walked change is a **gradient**: no fifth of a second of it clears 5 % of the picture, while the whole
walk changes 93 %. `settle` now takes a `span`, and `change_fraction` widens its window by it, keeping the
moment at the change's start. Tests: 10 in `tools/demo/test_check.py`, incl. the control that a span over
a still clip **still FAILs**.

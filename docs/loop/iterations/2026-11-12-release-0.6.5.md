Fixes: freed /tmp, `tools/loop/fleet/predone.sh` takes a host lock (`/tmp/fleet/big-heavy.lock`), the gate runs as a detached retrying unit, and workers' ad hoc builds take the lock shared while whole-suite runs take it exclusive.

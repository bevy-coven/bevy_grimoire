### Capture cap: 15 fps (`4538240`, `db60e4c`)

- Measured on a fresh editor, town (727 placements), 3 s sequences, two runs each, frames' `actual_t_s` spacing against the target. Rule (from the brief): the highest rate whose mean timing error is under a quarter of the frame interval.

| Rate | Mean error (run 1 / run 2) | Limit (¼ interval) | Sustained |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 fps | 18.3 / 9.8 ms | 31.2 ms | yes |
| 15 fps | 9.9 / 14.4 ms | 16.7 ms | yes |
| 20 fps | 12.6 / 18.0 ms | 12.5 ms | no (all frames written at 50 ms gap) |

- Cap: `MAX_FPS = 15`. Rates above it are refused, not clamped; test `a_sequence_refuses_sixteen_frames_a_second`. `a_sequence_takes_eight_frames_a_second` is the other end.
- Correction to `db60e4c`: its table listed 20 fps as sustained at 9.5 ms, and 8.4 / 7.1 ms. Those figures did not reproduce on the fresh editor. The 30 fps row (one pass, one fail) and the 40 fps row (the editor stopped answering while the sequencer stayed busy) came from the same run and are not re-measured here.
- Perf A/B: not run. The change is to the capture sequencer's validation, not the frame loop.

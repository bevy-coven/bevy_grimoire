## Why it is blocked

Making one press open re-pins those assertions, which the protected evaluator does not let a pass do, so the fix is the owner's to make or to authorise.

Neither #216 (the launcher) nor #295 (the Projects home) asks for two presses; the choose-then-open step arrived with the implementation. `Launcher::selected` exists only to drive that step and the rail (`mark_the_selection`).

## The question, and a recommendation

**#344**: should one press open a project card? Options, as filed:

1. Keep two presses (today): a press cannot open a project by accident; a person who does not know clicks twice.
2. **Recommended: one press opens.** `New project`, `Open folder…`, `Open the example` and `Take the tour` all act on one press, and so does a card from the keyboard. Opening is not destructive. `Launcher::selected` and `mark_the_selection` go; the rail stays for focus.
3. Open on a double press, choose on one.

The assumed decision is option 1 until the owner says (nothing was changed).

# 2026-11-03 shell: the inspector's buttons were under the viewport; four issues closed

Focus: UI shell (#224) · Commits: d786f11..HEAD · Machine: big · Outcome: shipped

## Summary
The inspector's `23:00` button "could not be clicked" for at least six passes. Pass 382 read the picker's `HoverMap` after a click at the button's own centre: the entity under the pointer was the **viewport**. The shell's left pane was 204 px (`SplitFraction 0.22`) around a panel that asks for 380 (456 on screen), so the pane clipped the panel and the viewport was drawn over the rest. The fix sizes the pane to the panel, takes the viewport's half of the window out of the docks, makes a dragged dock take its panel with it, and adds the recorder steps that found and then proved it. Cluster: #282, #283, #224, #255 closed; #258 not taken (see below); #284 filed.

Must-fix: blocks yes (ENVIRONMENT's buttons, and the right half of every control in the inspector), common yes, no workaround yes (the controls were unreachable).
Root cause: `SplitFraction(0.22)` of the body. Regression check: `a_pane_in_the_left_dock_can_be_clicked::the_left_pane_is_at_least_as_wide_as_the_panel_in_it` (failed before: panel to x=438, pane ends at x=204; passes). A first draft in the tree blamed `Pickable::IGNORE`; `bevy_ui`'s `picking_backend.rs` does not stop at an ignoring ancestor, and a build that made the pane hoverable left the click dead, so it was withdrawn.

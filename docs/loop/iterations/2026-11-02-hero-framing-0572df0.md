## Two renderer facts that cost the takes
- **`render.py` defaults `scale` to 2** (`rec.get("scale", 2)`), and the crop is multiplied by it. A storyboard
  that omits `scale` crops 2560×1406 out of a 1280×720 capture, so the editor renders as a small island in
  black and `check.py` reads 15 % scene. Set `"scale": 1` with a crop that matches the capture. This is why
  every sky take read 15–16 % scene while the raw frame was 68 % bright.
- **`family.ron` is at the project root** (`<root>/family.ron`), and a crate's settings live in
  `<root>/family/<crate>.ron` — not under `assets/grimoire/`. With them misplaced, `family_set` answers
  *"bevy_elements is not listed in family.ron, so its settings were not changed"*, and every sky operation
  is a no-op however right the file looks.

## Bugs fixed

### #277: the status band's job item does not answer a click in the running editor

Must-fix: blocks y (it is the gesture #223's acceptance names), common y (every `click_label` step),
no workaround y — a storyboard aimed by label could not hit anything.

Root cause: `tools/demo/record.py`'s `widget_centre` multiplied `UiGlobalTransform`'s translation by
the node's `inverse_scale_factor` (0.833 at the editor's 1.2 UI scale), on the theory that the
transform reports physical pixels. **It does not.** Measured on a live 1280×720 window: the band's
`labeler: not configured` text reports raw y = 705.5, inside that window; scaled it would be 587.5,
which is not where it is drawn. The multiplication moved every click up and left — off the window for
the palette's rows at x ≈ 1122.

Evidence, before and after, on the running editor: clicking the raw centre of five palette rows armed
exactly those five pieces (`pipe`, `pipe_corner`, `toilet_roll_holder`, `towel_holder`, `bath`); clicking
the band at its raw `(113.5, 705)` during a bake opened the popover, whose `Stop: Baking previews
12 of 90` row ended it. The #223 clip is re-recorded through that real click.

Regression check: `tools/demo/test_record.py::test_the_scale_factor_does_not_move_the_click` —
fails at `(935, 244)` against the expected `(1122, 293)`; passes after. The old fixture hid it by
hard-coding `inverse_scale_factor: 1.0`.

## Review

Reviewer could not run (no subagent spawned inside the budget). The five questions, answered by me:

1. **Does the fix hold for a widget that is not the band?** Yes — five palette rows, by name.
2. **Could the scale factor ever be right?** Not for `UiGlobalTransform`: the raw y of a bottom-band
   text is inside the window, so the space is already the input's. Stated in the code.
3. **Is the new test capable of failing?** Yes, and it was made to: reintroducing the old mapping
   fails it with the exact message quoted above.
4. **Does the key correction remove a capability?** No — `placement_remove` and `note_add` are still
   in the registry; the test asserts that.
5. **Why not migrate `MoveMode` too?** Naming `tool_arm` for it moves it from `Change::View` to
   `Project`, and the classification test pins the `View` set as an exact list. Editing that list is a
   re-pin of a protected assertion; the loop may not make it.

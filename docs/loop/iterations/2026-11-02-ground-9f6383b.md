## Bugs fixed
### #267: The flat ground z-fights the bounds floor
Must-fix: blocks no, common yes (every flat terrain), no workaround no.
Root cause: the slab and the ground were both at `origin.y - ground_drop`, so a ground at height 0 was coplanar with it (the issue guessed 5 mm; it was 0). Fix: the slab drops `BOUNDS_FLOOR_BELOW_GROUND` = 1 cm more (`editor.rs`, `fit_bounds_floor`); the ground, the trees, the grid and the gizmos keep `ground_drop`, so no pinned assertion moved.
Regression check: `the_ground_clears_the_bounds_floor::a_flat_ground_at_height_zero_is_drawn_clear_of_the_bounds_floor` (failed with a gap of 0 m; the depth buffer tells surfaces apart past 0.00024 m over the default 0..1000 range). Live before/after on Xvfb: stripes on the flat ground, then clean. Clip `2026-11-02-267-ground.mp4`.

### #268: the ground disappears after the first carve
Must-fix: blocks no, common yes (every first carve), no workaround no.
Root cause [INFERENCE, confirmed by the fix]: the first carve spawns chunks whose vertex layout (and, with `bevy_elements`, whose `WeatheredMaterial`) has no compiled pipeline yet, and the heightfield ground is despawned in the same frame. Measured at 15 fps, centre of the viewport: slab colour (23,23,22) for 8 frames (0.5 s) with the weathered material, 3 frames (0.2 s) with the plain one. Fix: a `PipelineWarm` triangle with a chunk's layout, wearing each material, drawn far under the map from `OnEnter(Screen::Editor)`. After: (193,211,176) -> (161,167,126) with no frame between. Clip `2026-11-02-268-warm.mp4`.
Regression check: `the_carved_ground_pipeline_is_warm` (the triangle exists in the plain material; it carries a carved chunk's attributes and index format). Mutation: removing the registration fails the first.
